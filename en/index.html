Living and working in Cómpeta

A white village in the mountains, close to the coast

Cómpeta lies in the heart of the Axarquía, in the south of Andalusia. A lively white village in the mountains, a short drive from the coast and Málaga.

More and more professionals choose to settle here, for a short or longer period. The climate, the peace and quiet and the international community make Cómpeta a great place to live and work.

Our office

Work, build your business and exchange ideas

Many entrepreneurs and remote workers work from home. That is comfortable, but it can lack structure, focus or informal contact with others.

A shared office space offers the solution:

  • a clear workplace outside your home
  • regular working hours and a good rhythm
  • the opportunity to exchange ideas with others
  • without the hustle of a busy coworking space

Working together here means: working independently when you need to, and finding each other for advice or ideas.

Office space in Cómpeta

For entrepreneurs building for growth

In the centre of Cómpeta we offer a small office space with dedicated desks. We are not looking for a freelancer selling hours, but for an entrepreneur with a business that can really grow. Someone to build and grow alongside.

This desk is for you if you:

  • have a scalable product or service;
  • don't depend on your own hours for your revenue;
  • want to expand nationally and/or internationally;
  • are looking for a sounding board of entrepreneurs who are scaling too;
  • enjoy working with a small, steady group.

No hot desk by the hour or day, but a dedicated desk among entrepreneurs who keep each other sharp.

Set-up and use

  • 2 dedicated desks
  • a steady group of entrepreneurs, no changing occupancy
  • a calm, professional working environment

The focus is on working together pleasantly, without obligations or formal structures.

Working, calls and online meetings

Calls and online meetings are part of daily use. The space is equipped with:

  • dedicated desks
  • attention to acoustics
  • working with headsets (not included)
  • clear and practical agreements

Several people on calls at the same time is normal and works well, thanks to our acoustic panels.

Frequently asked questions

Good to know

Who is this workspace for?

For entrepreneurs with a scalable product or service who want to grow their business. We are not looking for a freelancer selling hours, but for someone to build and grow alongside.

Do I get a fixed desk or does it change?

You get your own dedicated desk that only you use.

Are there ergonomic chairs and desks?

Definitely. We know how important a good chair and desk are. The desk is height-adjustable and the chair can be adjusted to your preference.

Can I make calls or join Teams meetings without disturbing others?

We are considerate of each other. In addition, acoustic panels dampen the sound, so you hear each other less.

Do I have 24/7 access or fixed hours?

The office is available 24/7. During the intake we discuss how you would like to use the office.

How much does a desk cost?

A dedicated desk costs €250 excl. VAT (IVA) per month, including electricity, internet and water. The minimum rental period is 6 months; after that you can cancel monthly.

Is smoking allowed?

Smoking is not allowed in the building.

Are there rules for using the kitchen?

There are no strict rules. There is a fridge for your own food and drinks that you are free to use. Please clean up after yourself.

How does the introduction work?

Fill in the contact form and we will get in touch to plan a one-to-one introduction. If that goes well, you meet the other tenant to see if there is a match there too. If so, you receive the contract and can get started.
