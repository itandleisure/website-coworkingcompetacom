Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains which personal data Co Working Cómpeta processes, why, and what rights you have.

Last updated: 24 September 2026

Who are we?

Co Working Cómpeta is a trade name of Frans van Dokkumburg (IT and Leisure Solutions), Calle Paralela Avenida Constitución 4, 29754 Cómpeta (Málaga), Spain. NIE: Z2699832M. Email: info@coworkingcompeta.com.

Contact form

When you fill in the contact form, we receive your name, email address, phone number, company name or website and your message. We only use this data to respond to your enquiry and schedule an introduction. The form is processed by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which forwards the message to our mailbox.

We do not keep your data longer than necessary: if you do not become a tenant, we delete it no later than one year after our last contact.

Email and WhatsApp

If you contact us by email or WhatsApp, we only use your data to answer your question.

Google Analytics

Only with your permission do we use Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd.) to see how visitors use our website, for example which pages are viewed. Your IP address is anonymised. We do not use this data for advertising. You can withdraw your consent at any time via the Cookie settings link at the bottom of every page.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct or delete your data, and to object to its use. To do so, send an email to info@coworkingcompeta.com. If you disagree with how we handle your data, you can file a complaint with the Spanish supervisory authority, the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (aepd.es).
